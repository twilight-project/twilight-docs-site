# Privacy

<p className="lede">docs.twilight.org sets no cookies and loads no advertising or tracking code. Last updated 10 November 2026.</p>

## What this page covers

This notice is about the documentation site docs.twilight.org. The main site has [its own notice](https://twilight.org/privacy). Other Twilight sites and services are outside this page.

## What this site does not do

- It sets no cookies.
- It runs no advertising pixels, session recording, fingerprinting or chat widgets.
- It loads no fonts, scripts or images from advertising or analytics companies. Fonts and scripts are served from docs.twilight.org.
- We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

## What stays in your browser

If you pick a tab in a code example, your browser remembers that choice in its own local storage. The choice stays on your device and is never sent to us.

## What we receive when you visit

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver a page, Cloudflare's servers process the technical details that every web request carries: your IP address, your browser and device type, the page you asked for, the page that linked you here, and the time. Cloudflare uses these to serve the site and to stop abuse. It gives us traffic statistics that are counted without cookies and are not tied to individual visitors. We do not try to work out who visited.

## Links to other sites

Links to twilight.org, GitHub and other sites are ordinary links. Those sites receive nothing from this one until you click, and their own policies apply once you do.

## Your choices and rights

Because this site sets no cookies and does not track you, there is nothing to accept or reject, which is why you see no cookie banner. For the same reason, browser signals such as Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track change nothing here: no visitor is tracked across sites, with or without the signal.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Write to [privacy@twilight.org](mailto:privacy@twilight.org) and we will respond.

## Changes

If what this site collects changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
